# Glossary

- **BIA — Budget impact analysis:** Annual affordability analysis from a
  specific payer's perspective.
- **CBA — Cost-benefit analysis:** Both consequences and costs expressed in
  money.
- **CEA — Cost-effectiveness analysis:** Incremental cost per natural unit of
  outcome.
- **CUA — Cost-utility analysis:** CEA using a generic preference-weighted
  outcome, usually QALYs.
- **DALY — Disability-adjusted life year:** One year of healthy life lost.
- **Dominance:** An option is strictly dominated when another option is no more
  costly, no less effective, and strictly better on at least one axis.
- **EVPI — Expected value of perfect information:** Maximum value of removing
  all decision uncertainty.
- **EVSI — Expected value of sample information:** Value of information from a
  particular proposed study.
- **HALE — Health-adjusted life expectancy:** Expected years lived in full
  health.
- **HTA — Health technology assessment:** Structured appraisal of clinical,
  economic, operational, and other evidence for a health technology.
- **ICER — Incremental cost-effectiveness ratio:** Incremental cost divided by
  incremental effect.
- **MCID — Minimal clinically important difference:** Smallest outcome change
  considered meaningful to patients.
- **NMB — Net monetary benefit:** Incremental effect times the threshold, minus
  incremental cost.
- **NNT/NNS:** Number needed to treat or screen for one additional outcome.
- **PMPM — Per member per month:** Common health-plan pricing unit.
- **PSA — Probabilistic sensitivity analysis:** Monte Carlo propagation of
  parameter uncertainty through a decision model.
- **QALY — Quality-adjusted life year:** Time weighted by health-state utility.
- **SROI — Social return on investment:** Monetized stakeholder outcomes per
  unit invested, including explicitly adjusted proxy values.
- **TCO — Total cost of ownership:** Acquisition, integration, operation,
  maintenance, support, training, and exit costs over a stated horizon.
- **WTP threshold (lambda):** Maximum accepted cost per unit of health gain.
