# Course curriculum

An eight-session curriculum for developers or analysts who know basic Rust.
Each session combines one tutorial, one runnable example, and a short decision
memo.

| Session | Topic | Practical outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Units, perspective, horizon | Frame a decision and identify admissible inputs |
| 2 | QALYs, DALYs, and utility | Calculate and critique a health outcome |
| 3 | CEA, CUA, ICER, and NMB | Make a threshold decision without ratio traps |
| 4 | Cost benefit and budget impact | Separate value, cash, and affordability |
| 5 | Screening and diagnostics | Explain prevalence, PPV, false positives, and cost per case |
| 6 | Sensitivity, PSA, EVPI, and EVSI | Quantify decision uncertainty and research value |
| 7 | Digital health and AI economics | Connect adoption and quality to realized value |
| 8 | Engineering economics | Prioritize delivery using delay, flow, DORA, and debt economics |

Assessment is a reproducible case study containing assumptions, source units,
Rust calculations, sensitivity analysis, and a one-page recommendation. Grade
the reasoning and traceability rather than whether the intervention is adopted.
