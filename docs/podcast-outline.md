# Audio and podcast outline

The versionable deliverable is a narration-ready script plan; recording and
hosting require a publisher and accessible transcript workflow.

## Episode 1 — What does “worth it” mean? (15 minutes)

Define perspective, opportunity cost, threshold, ICER, NMB, and affordability.
Walk through the 50-QALY example, reading every unit aloud. Close by explaining
why an economically attractive intervention can still exceed a payer’s budget.

## Episode 2 — Uncertainty is a decision input (15 minutes)

Contrast a point estimate, one-way sensitivity analysis, PSA, EVPI, and EVSI.
Explain that EVPI is an upper bound on the value of removing uncertainty, not a
promise that a study will deliver that value.

## Episode 3 — When saved time becomes value (15 minutes)

Separate wage value, capacity value, cash release, and dissipated time. Connect
the same logic to developer productivity and cost of delay.

Publish a verbatim transcript with any recording and verbally describe tables
or diagrams referenced in show notes.
