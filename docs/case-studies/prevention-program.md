# Case study: prevention programme

A ten-year programme reaches 100,000 people at £25 per person-year. With a
discounted-years factor of 8.3, programme cost is £20.75 million. Preventing 400
cases worth £45,000 in downstream cost offsets £18 million, leaving £2.75
million net cost. If each case loses three QALYs, the programme gains 1,200
QALYs at roughly £2,292/QALY.

The programme is highly cost-effective at a £20,000/QALY threshold without
being cash-saving. Use `prevention_economics` to preserve that distinction,
`number_needed_to_screen` for programme scale, and
`population_attributable_fraction` only when the exposure/outcome evidence
supports an attributable interpretation.
