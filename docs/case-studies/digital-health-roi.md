# Case study: digital-health ROI

An eligible population of 80,000 has 15% uptake, 45% activation, and 29.63%
completion. Each completer produces 0.03 QALYs and £180 of avoided cost. At a
£20,000/QALY threshold, value per completer is £780 and population value is
£1.248 million.

Compose `activation_and_uptake::value_per_completer` with
`funnel_population_value`. Then separate benefit lines using
`return_on_investment::BenefitClass`: only genuinely removed budget lines enter
strict financial ROI; monetized health and reused capacity belong in the broader
economic case. Add acquisition and retention economics only when their cohorts
and time periods match the value calculation.
